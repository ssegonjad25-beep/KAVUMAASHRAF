Automotive Diagnostics

How modern technicians use scan tools, measurements and technical information to find vehicle faults.

Data-driven workshop

Turning warning lights into useful information

Modern vehicles contain control modules and sensors that monitor engine, transmission, braking, safety and comfort systems. Diagnostic trouble codes provide clues, but they do not automatically identify the failed part.

  • Interview the driver and confirm the concern
  • Check warning indicators and service history
  • Read diagnostic codes and live data
  • Perform suitable electrical or mechanical tests
  • Verify the repair and document the result
Diagnostic workflow

Evidence before replacement

A disciplined workflow helps avoid unnecessary parts replacement and repeat visits.

01 · Identify

Collect symptoms, conditions and vehicle information.

02 · Test

Use approved scan tools, meters and service data to test likely causes.

03 · Verify

Confirm the fault is corrected and the vehicle operates as expected.
