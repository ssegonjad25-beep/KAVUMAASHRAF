Electric & Hybrid Vehicles

Understand the growing role of high-voltage systems, batteries, electric motors and technician training.

The next generation

New vehicles require new workshop skills

Hybrid and electric vehicles combine mechanical systems with power electronics, battery management and high-voltage components. Technicians need specific training and safety procedures before working on these systems.

  • Battery pack and battery-management concepts
  • Electric motors, inverters and power electronics
  • Regenerative braking and thermal management
  • Charging systems and onboard diagnostics
  • High-voltage awareness and approved isolation procedures
