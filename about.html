About American Automotive Mechanics

A learning and information platform celebrating the people, skills and technology behind automotive service in the United States.

Who we are

Built around knowledge, craftsmanship and trust

American Automotive Mechanics is a demonstration website about the automotive repair profession in the United States. It introduces workshop services, technician skills, vehicle technology and responsible vehicle ownership.

Automotive technicians work in independent repair shops, dealerships, fleet maintenance facilities, specialty workshops and many other settings. Their work combines hands-on experience, technical information, communication and continuing education.
